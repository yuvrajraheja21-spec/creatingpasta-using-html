Vegetable Pasta

A delicious and easy vegetable pasta made with fresh vegetables, pasta, and a flavorful tomato sauce.

Total Time: 30 minutes

Ingredients

  • 200g pasta
  • 1 cup chopped tomatoes
  • 1/2 cup bell peppers
  • 1/2 cup onions
  • 2 cloves garlic
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Preparation

  1. Boil the pasta in salted water until tender, then drain it.

    10 minutes

  2. Heat olive oil in a pan and sauté the onions and garlic.

    5 minutes

  3. Add the tomatoes and bell peppers and cook until softened.

    8 minutes

  4. Add the cooked pasta, season with salt and pepper, and mix well.

    5 minutes

  5. Serve the pasta hot and enjoy.

    2 minutes
